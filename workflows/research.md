# Research Workflow

1. Define the question, timeframe, geography, and decision context.
2. Search authoritative primary sources first; use independent corroboration for consequential claims.
3. Record URLs, publication dates, access dates, and relevant excerpts.
4. Separate facts from interpretation and assumptions.
5. Resolve disagreements and identify evidence gaps.
6. Deliver a concise synthesis, implications, and follow-up research needs.
